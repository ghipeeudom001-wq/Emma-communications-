About Hilopek

A beauty experience built around how you want to feel.

Hilopek Salon & Spa is a unisex beauty destination in Uyo created around comfort, care and personal expression. From hair and grooming to nails, makeup, spa treatments and more, the goal is simple: give every client an experience that feels considered from the moment they arrive.

Hilopek's own brand language centers on Luxury, Unique, Quality, while its public pages highlight comfort, hospitality and memorable client experiences.
